## Reconciled conclusion

SpeakSharp has an implemented differentiator and a coherent value proposition. It does not yet have a proven competitive advantage or moat. The immediate evidence gap is not another generic coaching feature: it is customer validation and qualification of the already-built repeat-practice loop—who accepts an action, who returns, whether a comparable outcome moves, whether that predicts retention, and whether privacy-sensitive users pay.

That does **not** silently reorder the Product Owner's MVP sequence. #1304 model selection remains the active release blocker; Stage-B, #1259 telemetry qualification, billing qualification and #1258 still follow the approved release order. Strategic importance and execution order are separate decisions.

## Documentation corrections this audit requires

1. Use “Private STT audio remains on-device”; do not claim no transcript ever reaches a server.
2. State newest-two transcript retention and server-owned `transcript_state` wherever persistence is described.
3. Describe Progress and the recommendation-attempt-outcome loop as implemented; describe retention/efficacy as unproven.
4. Replace the stale score-consumer inventory with a live-import and live-render inventory.
5. Describe guided waitlist as backend-only until a frontend entry exists.
6. Treat Focus Points as shipped partial rehearsal functionality, not the full Executive Rehearsal product.
7. Keep parity features, evidence machinery and local-inference cost hypotheses out of moat claims unless customer/economic evidence supports them.
