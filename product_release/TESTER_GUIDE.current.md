# SpeakSharp Beta Tester Guide

SpeakSharp is a private speaking-practice coach. Choose **Open Mic** for an unstructured take or **Focus Points** when specific ideas must land. Every customer recording uses **Private transcription on your device**; speech audio is not uploaded to a Browser or Cloud transcription provider.

The controlled beta is free. No card or checkout is required.

## Ten-minute walkthrough

1. Create an account and choose Open Mic or Focus Points.
2. On first use, allow the one-time Private model setup, then wait for **Ready — speak now**.
3. Record a natural practice take and stop.
4. Keep the page open while the transcript finalizes locally.
5. Save and review the exact session: transcript, delivery evidence, and one suggested next action.
6. Repeat once using that next action.
7. Open History/Progress, hard reload the exact session, and check any available PDF report.
8. Use **Report Issue** whenever something feels confusing, inaccurate, slow, or broken.

## What to tell us

- Did you complete the loop without help?
- Was the one-time Private setup clear?
- Did the transcript preserve the beginning and end of your take?
- Was the next action specific enough to use in another take?
- In Focus Points, did “detected / not detected” feel truthful?
- What frustrated you or made you consider leaving?
- What one change would make you return tomorrow?

Do not include private speech content in a bug report unless you explicitly choose to share it.
