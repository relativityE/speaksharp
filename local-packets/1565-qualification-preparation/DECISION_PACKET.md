## Statistical rules to freeze

1. Freeze expected **clip x arm x trial x device x cache-state** identities independently of observed output. Successful empty speech is full deletion against nonempty reference and an explicit missing-speech failure; crash/timeout/acquisition failure is a typed reliability FAIL. Missing data/identity/annotation is HOLD where appropriate, never a smaller passing denominator. No failed row becomes successful empty silence.
2. Use3 declared warm paired trials. Within each clip/arm, average S/D/I counts over these3 trials, then pool those mean errors and reference words once. No mean of clip WERs and no independent-speaker credit for repeated trials. Report counts/point estimates before rounded display.
3. Resample **whole speaker blocks**, both arms and all trials together, with matched multiplicities. Use actual recording-session grouping for sensitivity where known; chapter IDs are only proxies. Silence uses its independent source/environment-session blocks. Pool speaker-level trials before sampling. Never sample decoder trials as speakers.
4. Pre-register the complete finite list K of inferential gate endpoints/cells after final slice/device admission, before candidate unblinding. Proposal: family alpha0.05, Bonferroni alpha_cell=0.05/K for one-sided bounds. B=max(20,000,ceil(100/alpha_cell)) seeded resamples, seed speaksharp-1565-qualification-v1. This supplies at least100 expected tail draws; it is Monte Carlo resolution, not sample adequacy. Descriptive two-sided95% intervals use2.5/97.5 percentiles and are explicitly not the one-sided decision bound. The retained legacy2000/utterance-bootstrap settings are not silently called equivalent.
5. Compute paired delta/ratio anew in each block sample. For a relative latency limit, bootstrap candidate/baseline-1 on matched observed endpoints with positive baseline time; absent/zero/invalid timing is HOLD, not epsilon division. Quantiles and counts are predeclared in CLI's implementation/readme and verified with fixed controls.
6. Minimum2 independent applicable blocks permits computing an exploratory interval, not qualification. With fewer, no CI. **An identical-output tie control is comparator calibration, not a power experiment.** A/A runtime repeats estimate baseline repeat noise; they do not identify v4-minus-v2 speaker-level variance or correlation. Before candidate opening, publish a preregistered adequacy analysis with justified external/pilot variance or a declared sensitivity range, effect/margin, speaker/session dependence, weighting and family-adjusted alpha. Target>=80% noninferiority acceptance under a true tie is a proposed design requirement, not an achieved result. If evidence cannot justify the design, enlarge/freeze the panel before unblinding or HOLD. Neither73 speakers, a degenerate tie bootstrap nor a passing degraded control proves power.
7. An observed valid outside-margin or critical/reliability failure is FAIL even if some other evidence is missing. A complete, within-limit point estimate with an interval crossing the boundary is HOLD. Missing unsupported measurements are HOLD, not imputed passes. Every mandatory arm/device/slice gate must pass; no pooled improvement overrides a failing slice.
8. Baseline-versus-baseline calibration and deterministic known-degradation controls precede qualification. Detect omissions, tail deletion, invented repeat, filler removal/insertion, critical-number/polarity changes, wrong Focus target, silence hallucination, latency inflation, missing/duplicate/same-omission-both-arm rows, identity/backend drift, disposal failure and next-take contamination. CLI owns the single implementation; this packet adds specifications/worksheets only.

### All-success and zero-event boundaries

An all-success useful/Focus/critical-correctness panel, all-zero observed error count, or identical paired outputs produces a degenerate empirical bootstrap. Retain its finite-panel result; do not report population correctness=1, zero population risk, a precise noninferiority bound, or adequate power from that degeneracy. If a predeclared unit is defensibly independent Bernoulli with a common success probability, the exact one-sided lower success bound after n/n successes is **alpha_cell^(1/n)**; the upper event bound after0/n events is **1-alpha_cell^(1/n)**. These bounds are complementary and use the same family-adjusted alpha as the gate. Exact bounds describe rates, not unobserved error severity or a count-per-minute rate.

Independent unit means the declared speaker/session block, not repeated decodes, multiple words/filler opportunities in one take, or two ratings of one item. Binomial precision/recall bounds are not automatically valid for clustered filler opportunities. Use a justified clustered method/adequacy analysis or HOLD the inferential claim; no inflated denominator. For paired all-success arms, a zero paired bootstrap delta alone does not establish the relative margin; a separately declared conservative marginal-bound construction or justified paired boundary method is required before qualification. Do not silently invent that method after outputs.

### What the p95 claim means and how many sessions support it

For each fixed physical-device/browser/network/cache/thermal cell, setup p95 means the **population95th percentile over independent fresh boot sessions under that cell's declared sampling protocol**. It is not the95th percentile of600 clips, three decoder repeats or a pool of different phones. Report the descriptive sample p95 separately (predeclared nearest-rank X_(ceil(.95*n))); it is not its confidence upper bound. Warm per-take latency targets a separately declared speaker/session-block population, with repeated takes clustered; boot counts do not qualify those tails.

Proposed distribution-free absolute setup-tail rule: sorted independent session times X_(1)..X_(n), select the smallest rank k with **P[Binomial(n,.95)<=k-1]>=1-alpha_cell**, then use X_(k) as the one-sided confidence upper bound for population p95 (continuous population; conservative treatment of ties). If no such k exists, no finite bound is supplied by the observed order statistics: HOLD. Failed/time-out sessions remain failures, never removed from the timing population. A valid observed critical failure remains FAIL regardless of sample adequacy.

Even using the sample maximum, coverage is only **1-.95^n**:20 sessions give64.15%, not95%. Nominal alpha=.05 needs at least59 independent sessions; illustrative K=40 gives alpha_cell=.00125 and requires at least131. These are minimum sample sizes for a finite maximum-based bound, not guarantees that the bound will meet the budget. K=40 is an illustration, not the frozen endpoint count. Before execution freeze K and **N_boot=2*ceil(ceil(log(.05/K)/log(.95))/2)** per device **and per cold/verified-cache cell**, balanced AB/BA. K=40 would schedule132 paired sessions per cache cell,66AB/66BA. Thermal/time blocking and between-session reset must justify independence; arm pairs count once, not twice. If the resource scope cannot support that design, reduce the stated inferential claim with Sam/PO approval or HOLD; do not describe20 sessions as qualifying population p95.

Relative setup-tail ratio means q95(candidate)/q95(baseline)-1, not p95 of per-pair ratios. CLI must validate a paired-session-block bound with sufficient tail support and a declared boundary method; the absolute order-statistic minimum alone does not establish its coverage or power. Unknown tail support or zero/invalid baseline means HOLD. Whole-product endpoint tails still require separately authorized product runs, with their own block/sample justification.

[Statistical planning arithmetic](statistical-planning-arithmetic.json) and [reproducible calculation](calculate_statistical_planning.py) compute boundary examples and an **assumption-only** normal-approximation sample-size sensitivity grid. It uses n≈ceil(((z_(1-alpha_cell)+z_(.8))*sigma_delta/margin)^2) for independent, equally weighted paired block differences. Its sigma assumptions are not measured v2/v4 variability; unequal pooled-ratio weights, slice overlap and clustering require the actual design analysis. No empirical power has been established and no second comparator is implemented.

## Zero-reference-word bootstrap handling — explicit

Classify from **frozen source truth**, not model output:
- Expected-empty silence/non-speech: lexical WER N/A; retain token count, decoded-nonempty flag, duration and source-session identity in the separate hallucination panel.
- Real filler-only speech whose Track-A normalized reference becomes empty: also lexical WER N/A, **not silence**. It remains required contextual-filler and usefulness evidence. Never discard its output or set WER0.
- Lexical panel: include only frozen clips with positive Track-A reference-word count. Resample its speaker blocks; every included block must have at least one such reference. For every replicate compute pooled errors divided by the positive pooled reference count.
- Therefore ordinary lexical replicates cannot have a zero denominator under a correctly frozen panel. If any replicate nevertheless has N=0, retain a named invalid-replicate/invariant failure and HOLD the interval/qualification. **Do not discard, redraw, add epsilon, set0 or report a percentile over only surviving replicates.**
- A required slice containing only zero-reference rows has no lexical interval; its source-declared silence/filler metrics must still qualify. This is not permission to mark a required lexical slice satisfied without speech.
- Silence resampling divides insertions by observed positive silence minutes, not reference words. Zero/unknown duration invalidates that estimate; no silent-row removal. All-zero observed counts can be reported as a finite-panel result, but a degenerate bootstrap is not a population no-hallucination guarantee. Where independent Bernoulli units are defensible, report the one-sided exact zero-event bound1-(alpha_cell)^(1/n); never substitute repeated trials/segments for independent units.

JiWER4 documents an alternative empty-reference insertion convention; this proposal deliberately keeps SpeakSharp lexical WER N/A and governs hallucination separately. The convention is versioned, never selected after seeing outcomes.
