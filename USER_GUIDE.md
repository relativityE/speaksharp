# SpeakSharp User Guide

SpeakSharp is one Private Practice product. Open Mic is the primary practice flow; Focus Points adds an
optional brief when you want specific guidance.

## Quick start

1. Create an account. The complete product is free for 30 days.
2. Open **Practice** and choose **Open Mic** or **Focus Points**.
3. Allow microphone access and complete the one-time Private model setup if prompted.
4. Start recording. Your audio is transcribed on this device.
5. Stop to save the session. When the saved session becomes ready for review, SpeakSharp automatically
   requests coaching; no separate generation action is required. Review the transcript, pace, filler words,
   coaching, History, and Progress.

Each recording has a ten-minute technical cap. Active-trial and paid accounts have no accumulated daily or
monthly recording-minute gate.

## Open Mic and Focus Points

- **Open Mic** is a clean, freeform take.
- **Focus Points** lets you enter a topic and points to cover. Detection indicates whether those points were
  heard; it does not judge truth or quality.
- Starting a new Open Mic take clears completed Focus Points review state from the new recording.

## Your 30-day trial and subscription

The complete product is free for 30 days. After the trial, the same product costs **$10/month**—there is no
feature-limited permanent free plan and no paid feature tier.

At exact expiry, you cannot create or analyze a new recording until you subscribe. You can still read prior
sessions, History and Progress; export reports; manage or delete your account; and upgrade.

Payments remain unavailable until SpeakSharp separately activates the commercial configuration. An
unavailable checkout never grants access.

## Privacy

Customer recordings use on-device Private transcription. Audio is not sent to a transcription provider.
Your newest transcript-bearing saved session keeps its transcript text. Older transcript text is being phased
out: once expiry is switched on for your account, earlier transcript text is removed while your session
history, practice metrics, and the structured next action remain. Until expiry reaches your account, earlier
transcript text may still be present. After a successfully saved session becomes ready for review, SpeakSharp
automatically sends your saved transcript text (up to a length limit) for AI coaching to Google's Gemini
model. Audio remains on this device. On-device transcription does not mean every downstream text operation
is local.

## Recording tips

- Use a quiet room and a close microphone.
- Speak naturally; pauses are useful.
- Chrome or Edge provide the most consistently tested experience.
- If the on-device model cannot initialize, retry setup or send us a message with Share Feedback; SpeakSharp does not silently
  switch customer audio to another transcription engine.

## Analytics

After each saved session, SpeakSharp shows speaking pace, filler-word counts, transcript, and one next action.
Progress compares like with like: Open Mic history remains separate from Focus Points history.
For a review-ready session, SpeakSharp automatically requests one **What went well** suggestion and one
**What to improve** suggestion. It must not describe a review as ready unless a valid review actually renders.

For support, use **Share Feedback** inside SpeakSharp.

## What leaves your device (#1367, verified 2026-08-29)

- **Your audio never leaves your device.** Transcription runs locally in your browser.
- **Your transcript is saved to your account** when you save a session, so you can review it and export a report.
  Your newest saved session always keeps its transcript text; earlier transcript text is removed once expiry is
  switched on for your account.
- **When a successfully saved session becomes ready for review**, SpeakSharp automatically sends your saved
  transcript text (up to a length limit) to Google's Gemini model to generate one **What went well** suggestion
  and one **What to improve** suggestion. No separate generation action is required. Your audio is not sent.
- **Your session measurements** (word count, filler counts, clarity, pace, pauses) are saved to your account.
